\chapter{Introduction}
\label{ch:introduction}
\section{Background and Motivation}

Short trips and local outings support leisure, social gatherings, sightseeing and exploration, usually within a few hours or one day. Despite their limited duration, organizing them requires balancing available time, budget, transportation, preferences and desired destinations. Coordinating these constraints can demand substantial planning effort, especially when travelers are unfamiliar with the area.


Digital mapping and travel-planning applications simplify destination discovery, directions and travel information. However, a complete outing still requires users to combine multiple sources, select activities, arrange them and assess whether the schedule is realistic. These decisions become harder when planning time is limited or destinations have not yet been chosen.


A suitable itinerary can also become impractical during a trip. Delays, changes in activity duration, or decisions to add or skip destinations affect the remaining schedule. Users must then reconsider their available time, routes and priorities to keep the outing feasible.


These challenges motivate Trippa, a smart short-trip planning system that simplifies itinerary creation and management. It aims to provide an accessible planning experience that respects practical constraints and supports adjustments as circumstances change.


\section{Problem Statement}

The project addresses the difficulty of creating and maintaining realistic short-trip itineraries while keeping the user's planning effort low.


Users may know their available time and budget without knowing suitable destinations. Finding places, estimating travel, arranging activities and checking compatibility takes time. Attractive destinations alone do not guarantee a practical outing: travel time, opening hours and visit durations must also fit together.


Actual trip progress can differ from the planned itinerary. Changes during an outing may invalidate the schedule, leaving users to determine which remaining activities are still possible.


Trippa addresses two related needs: generating an itinerary that satisfies the user's initial constraints and adjusting it when the outing deviates from the plan.


\section{Project Objectives}

Trippa's primary objective is to help users create, manage and follow practical short-trip itineraries with minimal manual effort.


The project aims to achieve the following objectives:


\begin{enumerate}
\item Simplify trip planning: Allow users to create itineraries using basic information such as available time, location, budget, transportation, and personal preferences.
\item Generate feasible itineraries: Produce organized schedules that consider travel durations, activity durations, opening hours, and other relevant constraints.
\item Support flexible customization: Enable users to review and modify generated itineraries according to their needs.
\item Assist during trip execution: Provide a way to follow trip progress and adjust remaining activities when unexpected changes occur.
\item Improve accessibility and usability: Offer an intuitive interface that supports convenient planning and trip management across desktop and mobile devices.
\end{enumerate}


These objectives reduce planning complexity while improving itinerary practicality and flexibility.


\section{Project Scope}

Trippa targets urban local outings and single-day trips for individuals or small groups, including recreational activities, casual outings and visits to multiple destinations.


The system covers collecting trip requirements, generating an itinerary, reviewing and modifying it, and following the plan. It also supports adjustments to unfinished activities during an ongoing trip. Mapping and location information improve planning practicality, while optional AI assistance helps interpret user requests and interact with the planning process.


The project focuses on itinerary planning and management. Flight and accommodation booking, payment processing, complex multi-day vacation management and dedicated turn-by-turn navigation are outside its initial scope.

\section{Expected Contributions}

The expected outcome is a functional system demonstrating how multiple planning constraints can be combined into a unified short-trip experience.


For users, Trippa aims to reduce the effort of organizing outings, clarify the schedule and support changes during a trip. It helps coordinate destinations and make practical decisions based on available resources and preferences, reducing the need for manual planning.


The project applies requirements analysis, system modeling, full-stack development, geographic service integration and constraint-based planning to a practical problem. It also examines how optional AI interactions can complement a structured planning system.
