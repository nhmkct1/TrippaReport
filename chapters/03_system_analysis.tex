\chapter{System Analysis}
\label{ch:system_analysis}
\section{Introduction}

This chapter presents the functional and non-functional requirements of Trippa, a short-trip planning system designed to help users create, manage, and execute realistic itineraries for local outings within a single day.


As discussed in Chapter~\ref{ch:existing_system_analysis}, existing travel-planning applications provide various capabilities, including destination discovery, route optimization, itinerary management, and AI-assisted recommendations. However, Trippa focuses on a more specific problem: helping users determine what they can realistically do within a limited amount of time and budget, even when they have not selected their destinations in advance.


Trippa supports the complete lifecycle of a short trip, from defining travel constraints and generating an itinerary to modifying the plan, tracking actual progress, and recovering from unexpected schedule deviations.


The requirements are classified into two categories:


\begin{itemize}
\item Functional Requirements (FR): Describe the functions, operations, and behaviors the system must provide.
\item Non-Functional Requirements (NFR): Describe the quality attributes and operational constraints the system must satisfy.
\end{itemize}


The requirements also distinguish between the core Web App, its responsive mobile interface, and optional AI-assisted capabilities. This distinction helps establish a realistic implementation scope while supporting future system extensions.

\subsection{Out-of-Scope Functionalities}

The following capabilities are outside the initial implementation scope:


\begin{itemize}
\item Flight and accommodation booking.
\item Payment processing and reservation management.
\item Complex multi-day vacation planning.
\item Built-in turn-by-turn navigation.
\item Continuous background GPS tracking.
\item Real-time collaborative itinerary editing.
\item Autonomous booking agents.
\item Native mobile applications with independent business logic.
\item Offline-first trip synchronization.
\end{itemize}


These features may be considered in future versions but are not required to achieve the primary objectives of Trippa.


\subsection{System Boundaries}

Trippa is responsible for collecting trip requirements, generating itineraries, validating their feasibility, managing trip records, and supporting itinerary adjustments.


External mapping and place-information providers supply geographic data, place details, and route estimates. Trippa uses this information but does not implement its own geographic database or navigation engine.


AI services, when enabled, assist with interpreting natural-language requests and producing structured planning suggestions. The core planning system remains responsible for validating proposed changes against the applicable constraints.
